Frequently asked questions

What is a digest?

A digest is a single email that groups the newest posts from every feed you follow, organised by folder, with short summaries. You choose how often it arrives.

Can I import my existing feeds?

Yes. Upload an OPML file exported from Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire or any other reader and your folders come with it.

How do newsletters work?

Each account gets a private forwarding address. Subscribe to newsletters with it and Digestly pulls the content into your digest and reader, leaving your personal inbox clean.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — follow up to 25 feeds with a daily digest, free forever. Pro adds unlimited feeds, AI summaries, rules, instant alerts and the API.

Can I cancel any time?

Absolutely. Cancel in two clicks from Plan & billing, and export all your data whenever you like.
